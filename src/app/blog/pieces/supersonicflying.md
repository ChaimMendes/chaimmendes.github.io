We, the aerospace community, want to give you a supersonic jet. We can make it safe, silent, economical, environmentaly sustainable and whatever else you want. But we can't and here is why. 

## Physical limitations

Physicist or not, we still selectively hate thermodynamics, and sonic boom is… evil…  We can make these sonic booms into sonic thumps(we are smart like that) and pretty-much silent flight is possible at about Mach 1.3, but, at Mach 2 and beyond, we can't help air’s booming personality. So, we can only go at such speeds over deserts and oceans.

And, the 6 people in the desert also complain, so oceans(where there aren't any boats or islands).

Essentially, our supersonic airliners will be good for London to Miami, Singapore to Doha, Las Vegas to Japan, and other long-haul over-sea(Not over-seas, over-the-seas) trips, and it gets worse.

## You won't buy tickets.

For there to be any sense in buying a supersonic jet, you would have to sell tickets at business class prices. Ok. People will buy. But the problem is, when you pay for business class, you pay more for the “resort” and less for the "journey". So, if I told you that you stay in the resort is now shorter, you would say that the fare must be less. 

And, the service can't be worse than business class, otherwise you wouldn't pay our fares anyway. 

## You wouldn't go first class.

If economy is funded by business class tickets, supersonic economy will be funded by supersonic business. But if supersonic economy(at business prices) is sipping free champagne, supersonic business (at first-class prices) will have the following benefits:

1. There will be fold-out beds, but the flight will be too short to sleep on them. 
2. There will be food that will be freshly made, but the flight is so short, the super-economy food wouldn't get time to go stale either. 
3. You can tell people that you flew supersonic business, but first class sounds better anyways. 

In exchange for these great advantages, engines would be more expensive, maintenance would be more frequent, and airports would need modifications.
