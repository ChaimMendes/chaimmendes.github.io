For years, free software has tried to take on the likes of Adobe and Microsoft to create tools that are truly free. Open source software has seen some successes in a few places, especially in the arts. Krita has (ugly though it looks) been the industry standard for digital art for some time. Much less embarrassing, Blender actually did it. Today it's on par with Maya, and its UI is actually gorgeous. It only lags behind in paid plugins/add-ons used by professional studios that aren’t available due to licence issues.

Otherwise, we have our beloved LibreOffice. Please take a moment to let the embarrassment fade. And that was the closest we got to Microsoft. And Adobe; we were saved from embarrassment by simply failing to do anything. We didn’t even reach a ‘something decent’. 

And now there is this new guy.

## ArtCraft built everything.

LibreOffice went against Microsoft Office. Blender went against Maya. 

ArtCraft has gone against(I’m not kidding):  

- Adobe(Scroll down a bit.)
- Microsoft(The whole suite.)
- Figma
- AutoCAD
- Avid Pro Tools(A digital audio workstation, though I’ve never used one before.)

And their Adobe suite is no joke. It replicates:

- Adobe Photoshop
- Adobe Illustrator
- Adobe Premiere Pro
- Adobe Lightroom
- Adobe InDesign
- Adobe Acrobat

## They are packed with features. Broken ones.

Yes, they have replicated the functionality of all the basic parts of all those tools, but all the tools are young. A lot of advanced features are broken or buggy. But if you don’t want to give Adobe another million dollars, but it will do maybe more slowly NEARLY everything that Adobe could.

As a supporter of the mission, I feel compelled to tell you to report bugs on GitHub issues. [GitHub page.](https://github.com/storytold)

But even then, they are getting there. A new version is released nearly every day. Sometimes 2 new versions a day.

The ArtCraft launcher makes installing and keeping up with the constant barrage of updates super easy, and it is compiled for what I believe is one of the largest numbers of OSs I’ve ever seen software cater to.

## It’s free!!!

The combined cost of subscribing to all that they offer replacements for is USD1,160.87 / year or INR87,211 / year. Numbers vary due to regional prices, but wherever you are, converting USD 1,000 into your local currency and adjusting for the economic “forwardness” of your nation by up to 13% is a good approximation. (Numbers are calculated using AI and found to be close to accurate by a human.)
